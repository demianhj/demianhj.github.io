About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Technical University of Munich in the PRS Lab, led by Prof. Benjamin Busam. I am also a visiting researcher at the University of Cambridge with the CV4DT Lab, led by Prof. Olaf Wysocki.

I received my Ph.D. from TUM, where I also worked closely with Prof. Nassir Navab, PD Dr. Slobodan Ilic, and Dr. Peter KT Yu.

I study how models can generalize to novel scenes through multi-modal training, test-time adaptation, and efficient training and fine-tuning of foundation models, with applications in AR/VR and robotic autimation.

Research Interests

  • 3D Scene Understanding & Reconstruction
  • Vision for Robotic Manipulation
  • Multi-modal representation Learning
  • Open-Vocabulary Vision Automation Systems
